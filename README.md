# E-Commerce Sales & Operations Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

An interactive 4-page Power BI dashboard developed to analyze e-commerce sales performance, product performance, regional customer behavior, and order fulfillment operations across 2024–2025.

The project analyzes sales transactions, customer activity, product performance, and order delivery metrics to identify business trends and generate actionable insights.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Sales Dashboard
- Analyzed total sales, net sales, average order value, and total orders.
- Compared revenue performance across product categories.
- Examined monthly sales trends and year-over-year performance.
- Identified top-performing product categories.

### 2. Product Analysis
- Analyzed total units sold and product return rates.
- Evaluated average customer ratings.
- Identified top revenue-generating products.
- Compared product performance based on sales volume and revenue.

### 3. Regions & Customers
- Compared sales performance across geographical regions.
- Identified top revenue-generating cities.
- Analyzed customer purchasing behavior.
- Examined repeat purchases and high-value customers.

### 4. Fulfillment & Operations
- Analyzed order delivery, cancellation, and return rates.
- Evaluated average delivery time across regions.
- Compared sales performance across purchasing channels.
- Analyzed customer payment method preferences.
